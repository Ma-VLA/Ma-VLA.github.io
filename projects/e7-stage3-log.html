Eight heads, one counted

Attention runs in eight parallel streams, and the trained correction was written so all eight contribute.

The standard recipe pairs each head with its own share. Here the shares were meant to be added together first.

The recipe is chosen by a text substitution that never checks whether the result still means the right thing.

The two forms differ only by where one sum is taken.

The fix

Let the description decide.

The literature settled whether. It did not settle how.
