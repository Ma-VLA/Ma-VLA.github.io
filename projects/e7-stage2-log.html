Measurement design

The harness had to be measured before the policy was.

Hold the observation fixed, change only the word. Three things had to be settled first.

What the RoPE defect looks like

The bfloat16 cast reaches buffers, and the RoPE frequency table is a buffer.

Position Predicted angle error Measured K V
00.0000 rad0.00150.0012
640.00770.01030.0011
5120.06140.07370.0011
9670.11590.11670.0025

V receives no rotation and stays at the bfloat16 floor throughout — that is what identifies the rotation rather than the projection.

A buffer is not a checkpoint parameter, and the error grows with sequence length.
