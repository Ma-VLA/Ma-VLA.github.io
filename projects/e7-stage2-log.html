Each step starts from a state the previous step already got wrong. One forward is 12% short; ten land at a third.

It folds after all, with the right rule

The coupling factors out. Sum the second factor over the head axis first, then contract the rank.

ordinary  dW[n,h,d] = Σl a[n,h,l] · b[n,l,d]        → 5.8e-02
corrected dW[n,h,d] = Σl a[n,h,l] · (Σn' b[n',l,d])  → 1.9e-15

Exact at every layer, and it leaves the bundle's key structure untouched — unchanged serving code, no extra inference cost.

What it can answer

Restricted to the two categories that move, with a floor of one in two.
