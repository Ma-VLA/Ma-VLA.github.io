Single-owner boundary

One process owns each physical device.

Only ufactory_driver connects directly to the robot SDK. Only the three camera publisher nodes open their respective camera devices, one node per device — the two HIK cameras are held by separate processes selected by serial number. Every other process consumes existing ROS2 topics; a second direct connection would risk socket or device conflicts.

Current scope

This project page documents an operational teleoperation and data-collection infrastructure. It does not yet claim a trained xArm VLA policy, task-success results, or cross-robot transfer performance.
