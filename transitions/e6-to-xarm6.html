What transfers

  • π0.5 initialization and flow-matched action generation
  • Late-stage Vision LoRA hypothesis: SigLIP blocks 18–26
  • Action-expert LoRA configuration
  • Six joint-delta arm interface
  • 16-step policy horizon and external policy-server architecture

What changes

  • Binary suction → continuous gripper aperture
  • Dobot wrapper and statistics → xArm6-specific state/action wrapper and statistics
  • Coordinate-guided demonstrations → human teleoperation demonstrations
  • Dobot driver ownership → UFactory single-driver ownership
